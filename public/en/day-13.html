Behavioral Interview

Build your bank of 8 real stories today — this is the one day where you write the "content." Everything is saved in this browser.

~120 min Prerequisite: Day 12 Connects to: Day 14 — Mocks
Today's objective

Never improvise a behavioral story on the spot — you reconstruct it worse under pressure. Today you write the 8 stories you'll reuse (adapting them) for almost any behavioral question you get asked.

Format: STAR + Architecture Context
SituationTaskActionResult+ Architecture Context

The "+" is what sets an FDE story apart from a generic one: on top of classic STAR, it includes the specific technical/architectural detail — what decision you made, with what trade-off. Without that, the story sounds like any candidate's; with it, it sounds like yours.

Your story bank 0/8 with content
Interview questions these stories cover
  • Why this company? Why this role? Why AI? Why now?
  • How do you work with customers?
  • Describe a difficult architecture decision.
  • Describe a production incident.
  • Tell me about something you had to learn quickly.
